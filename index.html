About KATUN

Somewhere in every country there is a person who still knows how a thing was made — a pattern, a glaze, a way of forging or carving that nobody wrote down. Almost none of it was written down. The knowledge lived in hands, and it ends when the hands stop.

KATUN exists to keep the making alive, not just the memory of it. We find the object — in a regional museum, in a family chest, with the last person still working that way — record its ornament and where it comes from, and put it back into production with a maker who can carry it.

The record is exact: the object, the collection, the region, the motif. The piece is not. A maker reads the record through their own hands, changes the scale, the material, sometimes the composition — and we say on every page what they changed. That is how this knowledge always travelled. A copy belongs under glass; a reading belongs in use.

It started with textile in Montenegro, because that is where we live and where the first record came from. It doesn't end there. Clay, metal, wood, weaving, embroidery — different materials, the same method: find it, record it, make it again.

We're not a marketplace. We choose what goes on this site, we know every maker by name, and we take care of everything that isn't making: the photography, the writing, the translation, the shipping, the buyer. The maker makes. That's the whole arrangement.

Prices

All prices are in euro and include worldwide shipping. Customs charges in your own country are not included: parcels entering the EU are subject to import VAT from the first euro and, since 1 July 2026, to a small fixed customs duty. These are paid by you on delivery.

How an order works

You add pieces to the cart and send us the order by WhatsApp or email. We reply with availability and payment details. Your order is confirmed once payment reaches us — not before. Most pieces are made to order, and the making time is shown on each piece.

Shipping

We ship worldwide with tracking, and shipping is included in the price of every piece. Delivery normally takes 7–14 working days in Europe after the piece is finished. We send you the tracking number the day it leaves. If a parcel is lost in transit, we replace it or refund you in full.

Returns

You have 14 days from the day the parcel reaches you to tell us you want to return it. Write to katun.montenegro@gmail.com. Send the piece back unworn and in the condition it arrived, and we refund the price within 14 days of receiving it. Return postage is yours unless the piece arrived faulty or wrong.

Pieces knitted to a measurement you give us are made for you alone and cannot be resold, so they are outside the 14-day return. This is stated on every such piece before you order. It does not affect your rights if a piece arrives faulty or is not what we described.

Pattern charts are digital files. Once the file has been sent to you, it can't be returned.

Faulty pieces

These are made by hand, so small differences between pieces are normal and are not faults. If something is genuinely wrong — a hole, a seam that fails, the wrong piece — send us a photo and we repair, replace or refund it.
