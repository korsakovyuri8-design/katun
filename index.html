From a grandmother's chest

It started with socks my grandmother knitted. Things like these still lie in chests around the world, but almost no one is left who knows how to make them.

We find these patterns in museums and with the makers themselves, turn them into charts, and put them back to work. Not as a museum piece, but as something people actually use.

Home /For Makers

You make. We handle the rest.

You don't need English, a camera, or a shop to sell what you make. You need someone to do that part for you. That's us.

Join us

You're not on your own

Photography, descriptions, translation, shipping, talking to the buyer, handling returns. All of it is ours. You make the piece and hand it over.

Your name stays on it

Not an anonymous seller ID. Your name, your region, your technique - on the page and on the tag. People buy from you, not from a platform.

You know your share before you start

No listing fees. No paying to be seen. No money held for months. A fixed amount per piece, agreed before you cast on.

Where the money goes

We take the sale price as 100% and split it. Same for everyone, every country, every piece.

Materials7%yarn and supplies
Maker40%your work
Country coordinator12%the person running your direction
KATUN41%brand, promotion, logistics, shipping

At €45 the maker gets €18. At €90 - €36. At €170 - €68. The higher the piece is priced, the more everyone earns, at the same shares.

What we can't promise

We're at the start. There are no sales yet, so we can't promise you a number. What we can promise is a share you know in advance and conditions that don't change behind your back.

If you're looking for a guaranteed salary, this isn't the place, and we'd rather say so now.

Beyond selling

Your patterns become charts we sell - and you get a share of every chart sold, not just every piece. Later: in-person workshops in your region, recorded courses, and a permanent record of the patterns you work with.

Tell us what you make and where. We answer every message ourselves.

Thank you

We've got your message. We answer from katun.montenegro@gmail.com, usually within a few days.

Home /For Shops

For workshops and brands

You already make the work. We give you a page, put it in front of people who came looking for exactly this, and take care of everything that isn't making.

Talk to us

Your own page

Your name, your city, your story and your full catalogue — with a link out to your own site. You keep your brand. You don't dissolve into a marketplace.

We make your photos work

Send us what you have, even from a phone. We crop, colour-correct and prepare them for the site — and you get the files back for your own use.

Written and translated

Descriptions, captions and messages with buyers, in English. The barrier that keeps most workshops from ever selling outside their own country.

A physical point on the Adriatic

Fairs, hotels and museum shops in Montenegro. Your work stands on a table in front of a traveller who is already here — somewhere you would never ship a single piece to.

Nothing to pay to be here

No listing fees, no ads you have to buy, no charge for a place on the site. A share of what actually sells, and nothing before that.

Your patterns on record

We document where each ornament comes from and keep it. If a workshop closes, the pattern doesn't disappear with it. That's the part museums and archives talk to us about.

What we take, and for what

We take the sale price as 100%. Nothing else.

Your workshop47%materials and your work
Country coordinator12%the person running your direction
KATUN41%photo prep, copy, translation, promotion, logistics

You tell us the price you need for a piece. We build ours on top of it, so what reaches you doesn't shrink.

Where we are now

We're at the start. We're not going to tell you we'll bring you traffic — we don't have it yet, and you'd hear straight through that.

What's real today: the page, the photo prep, the writing, the translation, the fairs in Montenegro, and terms that cost you nothing until something sells. You keep your own shop and lose nothing by being here too.

Where this is going

A base inside the EU, so parcels from outside it stop being expensive and unpredictable for everyone we work with. In-person workshops in your region. And a growing record of the ornaments each direction is built on.
